# C-001 — The question, in one line

Theme: link to the direction and research context.

A question charter authorizes a question and a budget. The worker chooses the
experiments and derivations, and re-plans from the notebook after each result.
Use the requirement-list template instead when the outcomes are known in advance.

## Requirements

### R1 — The question

State the question so that a reader can tell an answer from a non-answer. Say
why it matters and what a useful answer would enable.

## What is believed now

Link the notebook claims and open questions this work is meant to move. For each,
say how confident we are and why. Include what the user expects the answer to be:
that is a prediction to check later, not a target.

## What counts as an answer

The kinds of evidence expected (empirical, derivation, source) and how far each
must go: for example, which baseline, how many seeds, which assumptions a
derivation may make. A credible negative answer counts. Say whether a claim must
survive an independent attempt to break it before the charter closes.

## Budget

Runs, hours, GPU-hours and spend. The worker stops when the budget is spent and
reports what is known; extending it is the user's decision.

A question charter is for open-ended questions, so a first answer does not end
it. Keep a running list of open avenues (theory and experiment) in the notebook.
Finish early only when every avenue has been explored, ruled out with a stated
reason, or needs something outside the charter's bounds; those become next
questions in the close-out. An independent review checks the list before
closing.

## Authority and boundaries

Repositories and permitted actions; excluded outcomes. The worker may design
experiments, build instruments, change its own plan and make the necessary
caller, test and documentation updates within those bounds. It stops for a
changed question or an action outside granted authority.

## Communication

Reader and assumed context. After each run: what changed in belief, what was
spent, and the next step. Name any finding that needs a digest before closure.
