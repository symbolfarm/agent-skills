## Operating rhythm

1. Interactive planning: agree attention lanes, cadence, decision budget, and
   only the execution capacity that is warranted.
2. Execution: jobs complete at most one bounded project-local task, commit
   locally, never push, and normally report locally.
3. Reconciliation: verify evidence and produce one concise weekly or fortnightly
   digest rather than forwarding every task report.
4. Human review: inspect milestones, publication claims, difficult-to-reverse
   architecture, or provider/cost/privacy/safety decisions—not routine commits.
