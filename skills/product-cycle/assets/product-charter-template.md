# P-001 — A product in one line, from the recipient's side

Uses the `product-cycle` skill.

## Purpose

Who the product is for, what they should be able to do with it, and why it is
being built (a client demo, practice, a hackathon entry, a tool for the team).
What already exists and may be reused.

## Brief

- **Recipient:** who receives the handover, and as what (user, developer, both).
- **Look and feel:** words plus two or three reference products or screenshots.
- **Must have:** the smallest set of requirements that makes it the product.
  Each states an observable outcome.
- **Wanted, in rough order:** a starter backlog. The lead may re-rank it and add
  to it within the purpose.
- **Out of scope:** what not to build.
- **Forks to keep open:** choices the owner may want to revisit. Build them
  behind an interface.

## Acceptance examples

Written by the owner: "the user does X, and Y happens." They are played through
the real build. Do not special-case their wording.

## Budget and team

- **Budget:** minutes, hours or runs, and any token or spend limit.
- **Lead:** the agent that owns `main` and the decisions.
- **Delegation allowed:** which other agents or vendors may build, review or
  playtest, and any limits on their use.

## Authority and boundaries

Repositories, permitted actions, and anything the lead may not decide.
Publication: none, or the surfaces listed in `publishes`. Paid services and
contacting people need explicit permission.

## Communication

What the owner wants to see after each run, and at the end.
